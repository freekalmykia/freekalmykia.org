The Foundation will provide small, targeted support for projects aligned with the Foundation’s mission in Oirat–Kalmyk language, culture, history, and research.

## Eligibility

Projects and initiatives aligned with the Foundation’s mission are eligible, including:

- Language-related programs or teaching
- Cultural or educational activities
- Historical scholarship
- Independent research on contemporary Oirat-Kalmyk issues

## Application process

Applicants may submit a grant request using the form below:

- FKF_Grant_Application.doc - [Download](https://s3fkf-public.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/docs/FKF_Grant_Application.doc)

- FKF_Grant_Application.txt - [Download](https://s3fkf-public.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/docs/FKF_Grant_Application.txt)

Completed applications should be sent to <board@freekalmykia.org>

Applications are reviewed as they are received.
